The project · an AI companion for Ish

Designed to keep Ish company while he works.

Wolfie is an animated AI companion: a young blue wolf with a quiet halo and a calm, curious personality. I wanted him to feel present at a glance through clear gestures, without crowding the workspace.

His values are dependable, curious, resilient, and lightly playful: the feeling of a friend who checks in while Ish works. Each animation gives that character a visual vocabulary—idle, greeting, moving, waiting, reviewing, and recovering. The assistant handles the conversation; Wolfie brings expressive company to the desktop.

A companion should feel like someone beside you, not another thing asking for your attention.
From personality to motion

Build a character one gesture at a time.

These loops are the heart of the project. Each pose translates a familiar companion state into movement that reads quickly, even at pet scale.

01 / everyday

Idle · at ease

A gentle tail swish, a curious head tilt, an ear flick. Present without demanding your attention.

02 / hello

Waving · hey there

A bright little hello, with the energy of a friend who saved you a seat.

03 / momentum

Jumping · up we go

A spring-loaded burst of optimism for a win, a new idea, or a fresh start.

04 / headed out

Running · onward

When it’s time to move, Wolfie gets going. Halo steady, paws quick.

05 / looking back

Running left · backtrack

Same confident stride, turned the other way. Every direction gets its own look.

06 / on the move

Running right · full stride

A clean side profile keeps the movement readable and his silhouette unmistakable.

07 / thinking

Waiting · one moment

Patient, attentive, and still on your side while the next thing loads.

08 / focused

Review · looking closer

His thoughtful pose for the careful pass: ears up, attention on the details.

09 / the honest one

Failed · shake it off

A sheepish pause, then regroup. Even guardian wolves have off days.

BONUS / all together

One character, a whole range.

The complete motion atlas brings his everyday and task-time states together in one little roll call.

These are the character’s designed animation states. Their appearance can be selected as a pet; they don’t independently initiate jokes or change how the underlying assistant responds.

The design process

Give the animation a character to express.

Before the loops came a point of view: what the companion should feel like, what details make him recognizable, and which gestures would make his presence useful.

01

A cool blue palette

Ice-blue fur and deeper blue shadows bring the spectral, moonlit mood. Small warm notes in the halo keep him inviting.

02

A halo, not a costume

A fine pearl-gold circlet with a cyan glint floats just above his ears. It reads as a little light, not a heavy prop.

03

Young, grounded expression

A broad muzzle, confident brow, clean eye shape, and alert ears give him a youthful, self-assured presence. Character comes from silhouette and expression—not a gendered checklist.

04

Consistent from every angle

Turns, runs, and close looks keep the same markings, face, and halo so Wolfie still feels like Wolfie in motion.
